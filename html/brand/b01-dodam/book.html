Grow well, one month at a time.한 달 한 달, 도담도담.

도담도담 — the Korean word for a child growing up sturdy and well. Hanji cream, pine-green ink, and one cinnabar seal. Each milestone a parent confirms is stamped, the way a passbook is stamped: a quiet, physical record of a child growing well. Korean and English are one voice, not a translation.

App rules

CanvasOne column, 16pt gutters; hanji cream page, warm white cards; no pure white, no pure black.
Lists52pt rows; the seal replaces the checkbox — empty outline before, cinnabar stamp after.
TypeNoto Serif KR for the wordmark and screen titles (both scripts); SF Pro / Apple SD Gothic Neo for everything else.
Controls12pt radius, pine fill, cream text; secondary is a pine outline; destructive is oxblood text, never a red fill.
MotionThe stamp lands: 120ms scale from 1.15 to 1.0. Nothing else moves.

iOS adaptation

Tab bar of five with SF-style glyphs; large title uses the serif; safe areas honoured; the stamp is the haptic moment (light impact).

Do / Don't

Do
  • Stamp, don't tick — the seal is the whole brand
  • Write KO and EN as one voice
  • Keep cinnabar for the seal and the app icon only
  • Let hanji cream be the page; cards are warm white
Don't
  • No pastel pink-and-blue baby clichés
  • No pure white, no pure black
  • No cinnabar text — it is an ornament, not an ink
  • No exclamation marks in system copy

Korean

Native. Apple SD Gothic Neo on iOS, Pretendard on the web (CDN), Noto Sans KR as the last fallback. Serif KR for titles in both scripts.
