A month is a moon. The book is thirty-six of them. Ivory page, indigo ink, and a moon that fills gold as the month's milestones are met. Quiet and observational — the voice of someone who keeps a night diary.

App rules

CanvasOne column; ivory page, white cards, indigo type; the moon lives in the header.
Lists52pt rows; a small disc fills from crescent to full as the row is confirmed.
TypeFraunces for wordmark and month numerals; system faces for UI in both scripts.
Controls16pt radius, indigo fill, ivory text; gold only for the disc and the today marker.
MotionThe disc fills over 200ms. Nothing else moves.

iOS adaptation

Five tabs; the month header shows the phase disc at large title size; dark mode is the brand's native state.

Do / Don't

Do
  • One moon per screen, maximum
  • Use gold for the disc only
  • Set month numerals in Fraunces
  • Keep copy in the past tense — things observed
Don't
  • No stars, no night-sky gradients
  • No gold text on ivory — it fails contrast by design
  • No exclamation marks
  • Never show two discs side by side

Korean

Apple SD Gothic Neo / Pretendard for UI; Fraunces carries Latin numerals only — Korean month labels set in the UI face.
