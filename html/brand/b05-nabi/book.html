App rules

CanvasOne column; sky page, white cards, 18pt radius.
Lists52pt rows; the wing fills mustard when confirmed.
TypeOutfit for wordmark and titles; system faces for UI in both scripts.
Controls18pt radius, plum fill, sky text; mustard only for the wing.
MotionWings open 200ms on confirm.

iOS adaptation

Five tabs; playful glyphs; large title in Outfit; dark mode deepens plum, mustard stays.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Draw wings from arcs only
  • Keep mustard for the wing
  • Short, curious sentences
  • Plum for action only
Don't
  • No cartoon butterflies or antennae
  • No pastel rainbows
  • No mustard text on sky — it fails contrast
  • No more than one wing per screen

Korean

Apple SD Gothic Neo / Pretendard for UI; Outfit carries Latin titles; Korean titles in the UI face at 600.
