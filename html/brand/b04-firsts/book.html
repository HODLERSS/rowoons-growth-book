Every milestone is a first. The app is a ledger of them: paper white, black ink, and one red circle — the mark a parent draws around a date. Editorial and exact; entries read like a dated record.

App rules

CanvasWhite page, light-grey cards, hairline rules; the list is the page.
Lists48pt rows; confirming draws the red ring around the row's number.
TypeNewsreader for wordmark and titles (Noto Serif KR in Korean); system faces for UI.
Controls4pt radius, black fill, white text; the red is never a fill.
MotionThe ring draws on in 240ms (stroke-dashoffset). Nothing else moves.

iOS adaptation

Five tabs in black glyphs; large titles in the serif; dark mode inverts paper and ink, red stays.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Date every entry
  • Draw the ring — never a tick
  • Let whitespace group
  • Keep danger in black; red belongs to the record
Don't
  • No colour beyond ink, paper and the ring
  • No rounded buttons
  • No decorative serifs in body text
  • No cheer — the record is the reward

Korean

Noto Serif KR mirrors Newsreader for Korean titles; Apple SD Gothic Neo / Pretendard for UI.
