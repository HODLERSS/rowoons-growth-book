App rules

CanvasOne column; cream page, white cards, generous 20pt radius.
Lists56pt rows; the leaf fills green when confirmed.
TypeNunito for the wordmark and titles; system faces for body in both scripts.
ControlsPill buttons, terracotta fill, cream text.
MotionLeaf unfolds 180ms on confirm.

iOS adaptation

Five tabs; rounded, friendly, familiar to anyone who has used a consumer health app.

Do / Don't

Do
  • Keep the sprout as the only illustration
  • Use green for done only
  • Cream page, white cards
  • Short, cheerful sentences
Don't
  • No gradients on the icon (the current one has two)
  • No sparkle or star motifs
  • No additional mascots
  • No coral body text

Korean

Apple SD Gothic Neo / Pretendard for UI; Nunito carries Latin titles, Korean titles set in the UI face at 700.
